# Interview Preparation Roadmap

## Recommended preparation timeline (8–10 weeks)

- Weeks 1–2: Foundation & basics
- Weeks 3–4: Backend core (C#, .NET, ASP.NET Core)
- Weeks 5–6: Data & persistence (EF Core, SQL)
- Weeks 7: Frontend (Angular)
- Weeks 8: System design, performance, architecture
- Weeks 9–10: Revision, mock interviews, real interview experiences

## Phase-wise learning plan

- **Foundation:** Core principles, language basics
- **Backend core:** C#, .NET, ASP.NET Core
- **Data & persistence:** EF Core, SQL, data modeling
- **Frontend:** Angular, UI patterns
- **System design:** Architecture, scalability, reliability
- **Revision:** High-yield review, mock interviews

## Mandatory vs optional topics

- Mandatory: C#, .NET, ASP.NET Core, SQL, system design
- Optional: Angular, cloud/devops, advanced performance

## Revision strategy before interviews

- Use revision sheets and interview question folders
- Focus on must-know concepts and common pitfalls
- Practice with mock interviews and real-world notes

## Long-term growth plan

- Add new technologies as your stack evolves
- Update real-world notes with new production experiences
- Expand interview questions to reflect industry trends
- Keep structure and templates consistent for easy scaling
