# How to Use This Repository

## Recommended study approach

- Identify your current level (beginner, intermediate, senior)
- Select topics relevant to your target roles
- Progress through basics, intermediate, and advanced folders
- Practice with interview questions and real-world notes

## Folder structure explained

- Each topic: 01-basics, 02-intermediate, 03-advanced, 04-interview-questions, 05-real-world-notes
- Root folders: Core topics, revision, mock interviews, real interview experiences, templates

## How to study a topic

- **Basics:** Build foundational understanding
- **Intermediate:** Deepen practical skills and patterns
- **Advanced:** Master internals, performance, and architecture
- **Interview questions:** Drill with real and mock questions
- **Real-world notes:** Learn from production scenarios and mistakes

## Daily interview practice method (30 minutes)

- Pick one topic
- Spend 10 min reviewing basics/intermediate
- Spend 10 min on interview questions
- Spend 10 min on real-world notes or revision

## How to revise before interviews

- Use revision sheets for each topic
- Focus on must-know and high-frequency areas
- Practice answering questions out loud

## How to add new topics or content

- Copy the standard folder structure
- Use templates from /templates
- Keep content concise and focused
- Update navigation files if needed
