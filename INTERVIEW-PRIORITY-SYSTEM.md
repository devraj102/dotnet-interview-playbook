# Interview Priority & Tagging System

## Why this system exists

- Prevent studying everything equally
- Focus on interview relevance
- Reduce frustration and burnout
- Enable fast revision before interviews

---

## Knowledge Priority Levels

### 🟥 Must Know

Definition:

- Asked in most interviews
- Expected at all senior-level roles
- Strong rejection signal if missing

Characteristics:

- Core fundamentals
- Runtime behavior
- Design reasoning

Examples:

- async/await behavior
- GC basics
- SQL indexes
- API design fundamentals

---

### 🟨 Should Know

Definition:

- Asked frequently
- Strongly improves interview performance
- Shows experience depth

Characteristics:

- Optimizations
- Internals
- Advanced usage patterns

---

### 🟩 Nice to Know

Definition:

- Rarely asked
- Bonus discussion topics
- Useful for differentiation

Characteristics:

- Edge cases
- Low-level internals
- Specialized scenarios

---

## Interview Frequency Tags

### 🔥 Frequently Asked

- Appears in most backend interviews
- Often used for follow-up chains

### ⚠️ Occasionally Asked

- Depends on interviewer or role

### 🧠 Senior-Only

- Asked only for 8–15+ year profiles
- Used to evaluate architectural maturity

---

## Difficulty Level Tags

- Beginner
- Intermediate
- Advanced / Senior

> Difficulty level reflects the depth and complexity of the topic, while priority indicates its importance for interviews.

---

## How to apply tags in markdown files

Provide examples such as:

Priority:
🟥 Must Know  
🔥 Frequently Asked  
🧠 Senior-Only

---

## How to use this system effectively

- When time is limited
- During revision week
- Before system design rounds
- For long-term learning

---

## Rules

- Every learning file must include:
  - Level
  - Priority
- Every interview question must include:
  - Priority tag
  - Difficulty level
- Revision files focus only on 🟥 and 🟨 topics
- 🟩 topics are optional unless time allows
