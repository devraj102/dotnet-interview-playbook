# Real Interview Experiences

## Purpose of this folder

- Capture real interview questions, follow-ups, mistakes, and feedback
- Track personal interview performance and growth over time
- Build a career memory log for future reference

## How and when to update it

- Update after every interview or interview round
- Record questions, mistakes, and feedback while details are fresh
- Review and update improvement plan regularly

## Why this is more valuable than notes

- Reflects real market expectations, not just theory
- Highlights personal weak spots and growth areas
- Enables targeted, data-driven preparation

## How it should be reviewed before interviews

- Scan for recurring questions and patterns
- Review past mistakes and feedback
- Focus revision on repeated weak areas and high-frequency topics
