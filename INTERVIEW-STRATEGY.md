# Interview Strategy for Senior Engineers

## How interviewers evaluate senior candidates

- Depth of technical knowledge
- Ability to reason about trade-offs
- Production experience and problem-solving
- Communication and clarity

## What interviewers actually look for

- **Fundamentals:** Core concepts, not just frameworks
- **Depth:** Understanding internals, not surface-level answers
- **Trade-offs:** Can you discuss pros/cons and make decisions?
- **Production thinking:** Real-world application, not just theory

## How to structure answers

1. Definition
2. Internals
3. Real-world usage
4. Trade-offs
5. Conclusion

## Common reasons senior candidates fail interviews

- Shallow answers, lack of depth
- Inability to explain trade-offs
- No real-world examples
- Poor communication or structure

## How deep is “deep enough”

- You should be able to explain internals, edge cases, and real-world impact
- If you can teach the concept to another senior, you’re deep enough

## How to handle follow-up questions

- Listen carefully, clarify if needed
- Answer step by step
- Admit if you don’t know, but outline how you’d find out
